Local AI and model cost reduction

Apple will launch a Mac app that simplifies running local models, Meta released the low-cost, high-throughput Muse Ember, and local LLMs have also been validated for use in game development. For developers building small tools, this means the barrier to on-device inference is lower—you can build offline-first productivity plugins and avoid API costs.
